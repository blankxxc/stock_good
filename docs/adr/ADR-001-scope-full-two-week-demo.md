# ADR-001: Scope for Full Two-Week Demo

Status: accepted

Decision: Build a two-week full-module research-platform demo. Core offline research loop targets L2; Spark/Flink/Kafka/ClickHouse/RAG/advanced models/simulation may be L1 PoC.

Boundary: The platform outputs cross-sectional scores, research candidates, diagnostics, backtest/risk reports, and cited hypotheses. It must not present deterministic trading instructions or guaranteed returns.
